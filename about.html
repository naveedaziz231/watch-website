Geneva Chronometric Legacy

AN UNBROKEN LINEAGE
OF SWISS MASTERY

Founded in 1888 amidst the snow-capped Jura mountains, Chronos Élite has dedicated over 138 years to the singular pursuit of horological perfection.

100% In-House Manufacture

Every mainplate, pinion, tourbillon cage, and hairspring is conceived, cast, machined, and hand-finished within our Geneva workshops.

Ancestral Hand Anglage

Our artisans dedicate up to 40 hours per bridge to achieve perfectly radiused 45-degree chamfers, an art requiring decades of muscle memory.

Strict Annual Limitation

To guarantee absolute uncompromised excellence, our total manufacture output is restricted to fewer than 50 timepieces worldwide each year.

Historical Chronology

MILESTONES OF INNOVATION

Key horological breakthroughs across our century-spanning history.

1888

Foundation of the Geneva Atelier

Master Horologist Henri de Montfort establishes the manufacture in Geneva, crafting bespoke pocket chronometers for European royalty.

1924

The First Flying Tourbillon Pocket Watch

Pioneered a cantilevered tourbillon bridge supported solely from underneath, offering an unobstructed view of the escapement balance.

1968

Ultra-Thin Perpetual Calendar Calibre

Engineered an astronomical movement measuring just 3.45mm in height, calculating four-year leap cycles with instantaneous midnight jumps.

2026

The Sovereign Skeleton Tourbillon

Combining Grade 5 titanium, 18K champagne gold bridges, and high-frequency regulation with connected bespoke collector stewardship.
