Haute Horlogerie Disciplines

THE CORNERSTONES OF VIRTUOSITY

Specular Black Polishing

Considered the supreme pinnacle of finishing: polishing steel components against zinc plates with diamond micro-powder until they reflect pure black from certain angles.

Thermally Blued Screws

Every screw is individually heat-treated over copper shavings to precisely 290°C, yielding an indelible, iridescent royal blue sheen that resists oxidation forever.

Sapphire Crystal Dome

Grown from pure corundum crystals at 2050°C and cut with diamond tools, with dual-sided anti-reflective multi-coating for 99.8% visual clarity.
